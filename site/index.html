  1. 01 / Overview

    One panel per account.

    Each linked account gets a panel: its 5-hour session, weekly pools, per-model limits, credits and banked resets.

  2. 02 / Detailed

    Closest to the limit, first.

    Every account in one list, sorted by how near it is to running out.

  3. 03 / Compare

    Which account to use next.

    Ranks the accounts of one provider by room left and names the one to use.

  4. 04 / Timeline

    When headroom comes back.

    Every limit window on a calendar, so you see the next reset before you need it.

  5. 05 / Wallet

    What it all costs.

    Subscriptions, usage spend and top-ups in one currency, with renewals.

  6. 06 / Alerts

    Told before you hit the wall.

    A message in the app, on Telegram or by webhook when a limit runs low.

No cloud version

Your logins stay on your server.

There is no hosted version, on purpose.A hosted copy would hold everyone's logins.So you run your own.

  • Only youOne owner signs in, with a passkey.
  • EncryptedYour logins are locked with a key on your server.
  • Look, don't touchIt never uses up a limit or spends credits.
  • Honest numbersA missing number shows as unknown, not zero.
  • Your callAutomations stay off until you turn them on.

Quick start

One command.

Free and open source.Runs with Docker on Intel and ARM machines.

SHELL
$ docker run -d --name headroom \-p 8080:8080 \-v headroom-data:/var/lib/headroom/data \-v headroom-secrets:/etc/headroom \ghcr.io/amitray007/headroom:latest

Then open http://localhost:8080.
