Dashboard Views

One set of accounts, four ways to look at it. Pick the view that fits how you feel about your limits today.

Detailed Default

What is going on in this account?

For the full picture. One panel per account with every limit, and the account settings.

Overview

Am I okay right now?

For a quick look. One tile per provider, with its tightest limit.

Compare

Which account should I use?

For people with several accounts. Every account side by side, one row each.

Timeline

When does capacity come back?

For planners. Each limit as a bar from open to reset, with next resets and saved resets below.

Later: Wallet. It will track what each subscription costs in money (plans, totals, budget) once planned.

How It Fits Together

Switch In The Top Bar

Every page has the same view switcher. Move between views in one click.

Remembered Per Device

Your last view opens next time on that phone or laptop. Nothing syncs.

Optional Default View

Pick a view in Settings to open it first, on every device that has no choice yet.

How each view handles several accounts
Detailed One full panel per account, as today.
Overview Accounts roll up into one tile per provider. The tile shows the tightest limit across them.
Compare One row per account, grouped by provider, so you can pick the one with room.
Timeline One lane per account under its provider, each window drawn from open to reset. Resets from all accounts merge into Up Next, beside a Watch List of low limits and saved resets.
