Headroom · logo exploration

1 · Ceiling

A fill bar low in the frame, a thin ceiling line at the top. The empty space between them is the headroom.

2 · Tile

A solid rounded tile. The fill is carved out of it as a bar near the bottom, and the room above is carved too. Reads at 16 px.

3 · Ascender

A geometric lowercase h whose ascender rises well above the arch. The word is in the letter; the headroom is the ascender.

4 · Ledger

Two bars of different length under one ceiling line: several accounts, one view. Closest to what the page itself shows.
