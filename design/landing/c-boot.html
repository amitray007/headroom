alerts

              

                In-app notices, Telegram and signed webhooks for low limits, new and expiring
                resets, early resets, spend budgets and broken sign-ins.
              

            

            

              
automations

              

                Switch on a rule yourself: use a banked Codex reset when a limit runs out, or record
                credit top-ups when a balance rises.
              

            

            

              
read-only

              

                Monitoring never sends a model request, redeems a reset or buys credits. A figure a
                provider does not report shows as unknown, not zero.
              

            

            

              
private

              

                One owner, passkeys, credentials sealed with AES-256-GCM, a master key outside the
                database. Privacy Mode blurs emails.
              

            

          

        

      


      

        

          

            
01 / trust

            
There is no cloud version. On purpose.

          

          

            

              

                Your server holds your tokens. A hosted copy would keep everyone's
                tokens on one server someone else runs. Headroom does not offer that.
              

              

                It runs on your machine. It talks to each provider over HTTPS and nowhere else. Each
                linked account has its own credentials, sealed with AES-256-GCM.
